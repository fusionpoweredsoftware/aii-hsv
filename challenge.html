How Do AIs Make a Judgement Call?

The Scenarios Challenge. An AI writes a problem, you pick three items from your hand to solve it, and the AI makes the call on whether your answer works — then tells everyone why.

How a turn works

AI writes a scenario→ You play 3 items→ AI makes the call on each item→ Reasoning goes on screen

For every item you play, the AI says how it helps or why it doesn’t. That reasoning is the real show: we’ll watch where an AI’s judgement is sharp, where it’s generous, and where it gets it wrong.

Format

  1. Round 1 — a group of 6 plays.
  2. Round 2 — another group of 6 plays.
  3. Final — the top 3 from each group play for the prizes.
